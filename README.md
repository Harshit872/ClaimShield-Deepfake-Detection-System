# ClaimShield AI

An AI-powered multimodal insurance claim investigation and evidence analysis platform.

> **Crucial Operating Principle**: The AI assists and empowers human investigators with explainable forensic findings and risk indicators. **The AI does NOT make final claim decisions** — final decisions belong exclusively to the human investigator.
